title: My first ever IAP sale
---

A couple of days ago, someone bought the Pro upgrade for [Basic Countdown Timer](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/basic-countdown-timer/id6692619076) today. That's $2.99, minus Apple's cut.

This is the first IAP sale I've ever made! I wrote the first version of this app on an iPad in 2024 to learn Swift. Then, I abandoned it for two years. It gets about 20 downloads per month.

Whoever you are, thank you!
