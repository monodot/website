title: Just ship
---
I just read this in an [interview with the makers of the app 'Grug',](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=ux44ymcr) which won an award at this year's Apple Design Awards.

> **What advice would you give to a developer or designer just starting out?**
>
> Ship. When you build, you find new ideas, bad ideas, technical roadblocks, better directions, and real people. Put something into the world, learn from what happens, then keep going.

So, just ship.
