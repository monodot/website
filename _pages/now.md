You can find the [slides here](https://speakerdeck.com/immavalls/shift-left-with-reliability-testing-a-hands-on-introduction-to-k6-7ccbce8a-0f70-4242-97aa-0c1c23fa9a0f) and the labs in [this repo](https://github.com/immavalls/cnd-norway-2026-k6-workshop).
