### Nice Move - Quick Workouts

An app for you if you've got access to an outdoor gym or bars nearby and you just want to move around, in a quick workout, based around callisthenics or bodyweight exercises. Syncs a predefined workout to an Apple Watch so you can leave your phone at home.

<p><a role="button" href="https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/nice-move-quick-workouts/id6748263452">View on the App Store →</a></p>

### WalkThere

An app for guiding you to a destination just by distance and bearing. This app is for you if you like walking in cities, but you don't like painful turn-by-turn navigation. Just set your destination, and hit the button. Get guidance on your Apple Watch, and enjoy walking to your destination. Don't forget to look around you!

<p><a role="button" href="https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/walkthere/id6758466851">View on the App Store →</a></p>

## Web Tools

Free mini tools that run entirely in your browser — nothing is sent to a server.

### Pension Calculator

This is a private, web-based pension calculator (in &pound;). It was built to answer the questions I wanted to know, like: how much money do I need to retire? What age can I retire at? What income could a pension pot give me, and how long might it last? Started out at as Claude Artifact (hence the quite nice design).
