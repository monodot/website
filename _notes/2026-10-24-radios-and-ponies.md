I’m looking for some inspiration for my own Apple Watch development aspirations, and I found these really fun apps (via r/AppleWatchApps):

[RadioStation](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/radiostation/id453459897) - free, private radio player with cute Apple Watch tuner companion app

[Crown Calm](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/crown-calm/id6822240252) - fidget app for Watch. I love the marble maze!

[PonyRelief](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ponyrelief-hrv-stress-trends/id6816663377) - HRV stress trends, but mainly just because you can choose your own pony mascot
