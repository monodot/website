I’ve just read on the great This Sceptred Dial radio newsletter that the BBC is to axe, amongst others, Free Thinking which is such a great show. I’ve discovered numerous interesting things from that show including [On the Silver Globe](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/m001vllj), a fascinating unfinished sci-fi film project I’d never heard of. 

And I really hope the BBC finds another outlet for Matthew Sweet. He’s one of the best radio presenters they have.
