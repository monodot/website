Assumptions & methodology

  • Drawdown income. Uses the 4% rule, an evidence-based guideline suggesting you can withdraw 4% of your pot per year with a high probability of not running out over a 30-year retirement.
  • Annuity rates. Estimated 2025 rates for a single-life, level (non-inflation-linked) annuity, shown below. Inflation-linked annuities start ~30–40% lower. Enhanced rates are available for smokers or those with health conditions.

  • State pension. The full new State Pension is £11,502/yr (2024/25). Qualifying age is 67. Requires 35 qualifying NI years.
  • Tax. All figures are gross (before tax). 25% of your pot can typically be taken tax-free. Withdrawals above £12,570/yr are subject to income tax. Consult an IFA for personalised tax planning.
  • Inflation. All figures are in today's money (real terms). Growth rates are after inflation.
  • Retirement budget. Default amounts on the Budget tab are typical annual figures for a retired household in the UK, in today's money. They're a starting point only, so edit every line to match your plans. Items marked essential (housing, bills, groceries, transport, health) are the floor you can fall back to; everything else (holidays, leisure, eating out, gifts) is discretionary. The "pot needed" figure uses the 4% drawdown rule against your total budget.
