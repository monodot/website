Hello! My name is Tom Donohue and this is my personal web site. I'm a Staff Field Engineer at Grafana Labs, and I live in London. I'm interested in software, writing, and egg sandwiches. 

This website is a place for me to share the things that I'm working on, my thoughts on side projects, and for random writings!

Currently my main activities are:

- Working at [Grafana Labs](https://grafana.com), where I help engineering teams get to grips with observability using Loki, OpenTelemetry, and the rest of the LGTM open source stack.

- Writing on [my blog](https://monodot.co.uk) about observability, side projects, and whatever I've just broken or fixed.

- Building small [iOS apps and web tools](/projects/) in whatever spare time I can find. See the [Projects](/projects/) page for more info.

- Building [Apoll](https://apoll.app), a simple polling and audience Q&A app that I sometimes use when I'm presenting a talk.

- Occasionally [uploading to YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@tomdxyz) when one of my side projects produces something worth showing, or there's something I want to share.

- [My GitHub](https://github.com/monodot), where I share code and stuff

- [Knowledge Base](https://kb.tomd.xyz), a general dump of tech notes and code snippets, which I update when I can.

This site is made with [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/).

PS. **Why monodot?** Because it's an anagram of the first 7 letters of my name.
