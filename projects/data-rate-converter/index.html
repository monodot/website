How it's worked out

  • A day is 86,400 seconds (24 × 60 × 60).
  • A month is an average month: 365.25 ÷ 12 = 30.4375 days, or 2,629,800 seconds. A 30-day month is about 1.4% less; a 31-day month about 1.8% more.
  • Decimal units (KB, MB, GB, TB, PB) step up by 1000. This is what network speeds, ISPs, cloud providers and drive makers use.
  • Binary units (KiB, MiB, GiB, TiB, PiB) step up by 1024. Operating systems often show file sizes this way, sometimes with the decimal labels.
  • Bytes, not bits. Everything here is in bytes. Link speeds quoted in Mbps or Gbps are bits: divide by 8 to get MB/s.
  • The rate is assumed to be sustained the whole time, with no gaps or protocol overhead.
